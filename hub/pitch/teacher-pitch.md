## What it is

It's a team coordination dashboard that shows the live status of all five components in one place. Each team maintains a small JSON file in their own repo. The hub reads those files and renders a status card for each team — what's done, what's in progress, what's blocked, and when it was last updated.

## Why I built it

The project has five teams working on separate components that depend on each other. Without a shared view of progress, teams risk building in isolation and only discovering integration problems at Week 9. This gives everyone — including you — a live window into where things actually stand.

## What you'd see as an observer

- Every team's current status at a glance
- A project roadmap showing which phase we're in and what's next
- Weekly update videos posted each week summarizing decisions and direction
- Meeting notes and decisions logged as they happen
- A progress feed so teams can post updates any time, not just at weekly meetings

## How we're running it

- Weekly minimum status update per team, with the ability to post progress any time
- Blockers surface at the top of each meeting agenda — low pressure, solution-focused
- Definition of done: working code, documented, accessibility checked with Edward, handoff-ready
- Disagreements go to a class vote after both sides post a short video — last resort only
- Edward reviews at milestones, not just at the end
